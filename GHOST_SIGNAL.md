# GHOST SIGNAL

GHOST SIGNAL is NerdSync's hidden, original cyber-noir narrative game. It follows Vesper, a courier declared dead after Ward Nine was sealed, and 312 people who have survived beneath the district while the city mourned them. Its atmosphere draws broadly from neon-noir and cyberpunk fiction without using characters, story text, or protected settings from another work.

## Entry

Signed-in users enter the game through the **Secret Find** link in the NerdSync footer. The link is a native keyboard- and screen-reader-operable button styled as a quiet footer link.

## Endings

- **Open Signal** — broadcast every survivor's name so the city cannot look away.
- **Golden Ghost** — enter Ward Nine and help Vesper lead the trapped families to safety.
- **Human Frequency** — deliver the manifest to the families above and destroy the floodgate controls.

Replays retain previously recovered endings. Recovering any ending unlocks the Golden Match Signal card and gold border inside Creator Match. Each ending displays the run profile, reward, and a cryptic final directive on screen. The game does not create or download a file. Recovering all three is tracked as completion.

## Privacy and persistence

Progress is stored in `localStorage`, partitioned by the signed-in Twitch user ID. It remains on that browser and device only. It is not sent to Twitch, Cloudflare, NerdSync, or other users.

Because NerdSync currently has no shared profile database, other people cannot see the achievement. A future opt-in D1 profile system could make earned cosmetics portable or publicly visible.
