Privacy & cookies

Your data stays yours.

Before you connect Twitch, here is NerdSync's simple data promise.

  • No selling. NerdSync will never sell your information or discovery activity.
  • No data brokering. NerdSync does not sell your Twitch data or send it to advertisers or data brokers. The only optional third-party lookup is the public channel-name request to TwitchTracker described below.
  • No tracking cookies. NerdSync uses only essential browser storage for your login session, settings, saved creators, and recommendations on this device.
  • No hidden database. Your Twitch token stays in session storage and your preferences remain in your browser.
  • Diagnostics stay local. NerdSync keeps a sanitized troubleshooting log only in this browser session so you can choose to download it as a text file. The log is never uploaded automatically.
  • Historical Discovery is optional. When enabled in Settings, NerdSync sends only the public channel names and Twitch category IDs for a limited set of strong Discovery candidates through its Cloudflare endpoint to request 30-day TwitchTracker summaries. Opening Creator Match details can also request that creator's public summary. Your Twitch token is never sent to TwitchTracker.

Continuing allows this necessary browser storage. Clearing your browser's site data removes locally saved NerdSync information.
